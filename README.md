<h1>🏥 Healthcare Management System</h1>

<p>
A Java-based console application developed to manage healthcare operations such as
patient management, doctor management, appointments, billing, and medical records.
The system also includes a secure patient portal for accessing medical records.
</p>

<hr>

<h2>📌 Project Overview</h2>

<p>
The Healthcare Management System is designed to simplify hospital and clinic management
processes using Java programming concepts. The application allows administrators to manage
patients, doctors, appointments, billing information, and medical records efficiently.
</p>

<p>
The project demonstrates the implementation of Object-Oriented Programming (OOP)
concepts such as classes, objects, inheritance, encapsulation, and collections in Java.
</p>

<h2>🎯 Objectives</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Manage patient details efficiently</li>
  <li>Store and access medical records securely</li>
  <li>Schedule and manage appointments</li>
  <li>Maintain billing information</li>
  <li>Provide secure patient portal login functionality</li>
</ul>

<h2>⚙️ Modules in the Project</h2>

<h3>1. Patient Management</h3>
<p>
Stores patient information including name, age, gender,
medical history, username, and password.
</p>

<h3>2. Doctor Management</h3>
<p>
Stores doctor details and specialization information.
</p>

<h3>3. Appointment Management</h3>
<p>
Allows scheduling and viewing appointments between patients and doctors.
</p>

<h3>4. Billing Management</h3>
<p>
Maintains patient billing information including amount and billing date.
</p>

<h3>5. Medical Record Management</h3>
<p>
Stores and retrieves patient medical records securely.
</p>

<h3>6. Patient Portal</h3>
<p>
Provides secure login functionality for patients to access their medical records.
</p>
